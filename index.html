Cubix Game – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Cubix Game is an accessible, screen-reader friendly 3x3 cube puzzle game published by Vision-Aid. This policy explains how the game handles your information.

Information we collect

Cubix Game does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The game does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email address, or location, and does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Internet access

The game runs entirely on your device and does not send any data over the internet.

Data stored on your device

Any game settings or progress are kept only on your own device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and they are removed when you uninstall the game.

Microsoft Store

The game is distributed through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft may collect information about your purchase, download and usage under its own privacy statement: https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement. Vision-Aid does not receive any personal information from this.

Children's privacy

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects no information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
